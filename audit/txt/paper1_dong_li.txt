To identify the tool wear in real time, this article builds the online
detection system and proposes an integrated image processing
method to measure the wear width for ﬂank face of turning tool.
First, the images of ﬂank face wear are collected by the image
acquisition system. Second, the collected images are cropped
near the ﬂank face wear, and the wear area and background for
the cropped images are separated by k-means clustering algorithm.
Then, the wear edge is identi ﬁed by the grayscale transformation
and edge detection algorithm, and the wear width is calculated
by Hough transformation. Finally, the cutting experiment is
carried out on MAG HTC200 CNC lathe to verify the validity of
the proposed method, and the results show that the identi ﬁed
wear width by the proposed method and actual measured wear
width for ﬂank face of turning tool are in good agreement and
show the proposed method is effective and have micron scale calcu-
lation accuracy. [DOI: 10.1115/1.4053919]
Keywords: wear width, ﬂank face of turning tool, K-means
clustering algorithm, Hough transform, online detection, machine
vision
1 Introduction
With the applications of sensor technologies in manufacturing
engineering, the intelligent online detection in manufacturing
process has been developed rapidly and produce great economic ben-
eﬁts. As the foundation of modern manufacturing industry, computer
numerical control (CNC) machine tools are the vital evaluation for
national whole industry. The detection status in CNC machining
has an important impact on the machining quality of parts. In the
metal cutting process, if the wear or breakage of cutting tool is not
discovered in time, there will be damage of work piece, machine
tool, or unstable cutting process [ 1–6]. According to the statistics,
the cost of cutting tool accounts for 3 –12% of the total production
cost [7], which is not small spending for the production. In addition,
nearly 20% of nonproduction time is due to tool wear [8]. It has been
reported that by using sensors instead of mechanical engineer ’ sub-
jective judgments, the tool cost can be saved more than 40% [ 9].
The online detection of the cutting tool can be divided into direct
and indirect detection. The direct measurement refers to manual
measurement of tool wear when the tool is unloaded from the
tool holder, or automatic measurement of tool wear by machine
vision, laser, and so on, where the tool does not need to be unloaded
from the tool holder. Indirect measurement is the way that machin-
ing signals are collected during the cutting process, such as cutting
forces, vibration, and noises, and the tool wear can be judged indi-
rectly by the signal processing method in which the linear or non-
linear regression is carried out between tool wear and signal
characteristics. The disadvantage of indirect detection is that the
collected signals do not re ﬂect the direct information of tool wear.
In the last decades, researchers have performed more researches
on the tool wear and tool life. Kurada and Bradley developed the
system that is consisted of ﬁber-optic light source and charge
coupled device (CCD) camera to capture the ﬂank face wear of
cutting tool [10]. Pfeifer and Wiegers discussed the research progress
of tool wear detection and machine vision [ 11]. In Ref. [ 12], the
method of wear detection is presented, in which tool wear was cap-
tured from a pair of images. In Ref. [ 13], the CCD camera is used to
measure the wear width for a drilling tool. In Refs. [ 14,15], Devillez
et al. used 3D images to detect the wear width. Wang et al. developed
the measurement system for ﬂank face wear based on successive
image analysis for periodically measuring in milling [ 16]. Wang
et al. presented an image processing method to measure the ﬂank
wear width, they ﬁrst used the binary image to obtain the general
edge points, and then the threshold-independent edge detection
method was employed for detail detection [ 17]. Huang and cowork-
ers developed the measurement system for the detection of drilling
tool wear by CCD camera and microscope, which can calculate the
wear area, average wear width, and maximum wear width [18]. Cas-
tejón et al. developed the online monitoring system based on com-
puter vision to obtain images of ﬂank face, and nine geometrical
descriptors are used to describe the ﬂank wear [ 19].
Although more researches have been done for the tool wear
detection based on machine vision, there are still some problems
that need to be further studied, such as the feasibility and stability
of detection system, darkness of light, interference of cutting
ﬂuid, the correctness, and precision of the image processing
method, which will affect the ﬁnal detection result.
In this article, an integrated image processing algorithm is pro-
posed based on the machine vision detection system in the
turning process. The wear image of ﬂank face is captured by
CMOS transducer, which is featured with the microscope head so
that the wear picture can be enlarged. The collected image is ana-
lyzed based on the integrated method, and the validity of the pro-
posed method is proved by the turning experiment.

Algorithm. Image segmentation shows that the image is decom-
posed into nonoverlapping speci ﬁc regions with unique properties.
Excellent image segmentation should have the following
characteristics:
– The regions are divided by certain characteristics (such as gray
and texture features), and the internal region is connected and
has not too many holes.
– There are signiﬁcant differences for the properties in the neigh-
boring region segmentation.
– Zone boundary is apparent.

2.2 Grayscale Transformation and Edge Detection. The
color image is transformed to the grayscale image in the process,
which is known as gray image processing. Each pixel of color
image is set to R, G, B three components, and there are 255 different
values for each component. The pixel can ﬂuctuate more than 16
million (255 × 255 × 255) color ranges. The gray image has three
kinds of R, G, B, including 255 spaces pixel ranges. Generally,
color image is transformed into gray image to easier calculation.
The description of grayscale image is the same with color images,
which still re ﬂects the overall and local distribution, chrominance,
and luminance characteristics. Grayscale image processing can be
used by two methods.
The ﬁrst method is to determine the average value of each pixel
of R, G, B, and then the average value is assigned to the three com-
ponents of the pixel.
The second method is based on the Y, U, V color space. The
physical meaning of component Y is the point luminance, which
reﬂects the brightness level. According to the RGB changes and
Y, U, V color space, the relationship between luminance Y and
R, G, B can be established:
Y = 0.3R + 0.59G + 0.11B (6)
By using the luminance as the value in the gray value image, the
second method is selected.
In the image, the boundary region is represented as the connec-
tion that indicates the end and the beginning of the feature region.
The characteristics or attributes of different regions are different.
The edge detection achieves its function by difference characteris-
tics between the object and the background of the image. The dif-
ferences include gray, color, or texture features. The edge
detection actually detects the position where the image features
change. There are many different algorithms that have different cal-
culation processes.
In this article, the “robert” algorithm is used, which is a gradient
calculation method based on difference. The gradient represents the
intensity of edge, and the direction of gradient is perpendicular to

2.3 Hough Transform. The Hough transform is the feature
extraction method to detect lines of image, which has been
widely used in image analysis, computer vision, and digital image
processing. It has many signi ﬁcant advantages including robust to
the extraneous noise due to the line integration operation. The
polar parameterization of the line can be expressed in the time fre-
quency domain, as follows:
x cos θ + y sin θ = ρ (8)
where ρ and θ are the distance and angle of the normal vector to
the original line, respectively, as shown in Fig. 2. The Hough
transform associates each point ( xi, yi), with the sinusoid in the
(ρ, θ) domain. If N points are concentrated along the straight
line in the time frequency domain, they correspond to N sinusoidal
curves and intersect at the same point in the ( ρ, θ) domain. The
integration along the line produces the maximum, and its coordi-
nates in the ( ρ, θ) domain are directly related to the parameters of
the lines. The Hough transform converts the dif ﬁcult global detec-
tion problem in image space into the more easily local peak detec-
tion problem in the parameter space. The calculation process is as
follows:
– Quantifying the ( ρ, θ) domain, and obtaining the 2D matrix M
(ρ, θ) that the 2D array is an accumulator, and the initial value
is set as 0, M ( ρ, θ) = 0.
– For each point on boundary, all the quanti ﬁed values are sub-
stituted into the ﬁrst step, obtaining the corresponding values
and adding 1 to accumulator, M ( ρ, θ) = M( ρ, θ) +1.
– Calculating all the ( xi, yi) and analyzing the M ( ρ, θ), if M >T,
the signiﬁcant line is de ﬁned. The ( ρ, θ) is the ﬁtting parame-
ters, and T is a nonnegative integer threshold, which can be
acquired in the image by the priori knowledge.
– Deﬁning the line in the picture by ( ρ, θ) and ( xi, yi), and the
broken part is connected [ 22].
3 Experiment
3.1 Detection System and Material. The online detection
system consists of hardware and software components. Hardware
system includes CMOS camera, LED light, computer, and other
components. The ﬂank face of the turning tool is picturized by
the system, and then transferred to a computer. The architecture
of the system is shown in Fig. 3.
The CMOS camera and LED light source are ﬁxed on MAG
HTC200 CNC lathe by magnetic bracket. The install has no
effect on the movement of each coordinate axis of machine tool
[11]. The shaft is machined on the CNC lathe as shown in Fig. 4.
To change the dark machining environment, the AFT ring light is
used.
The CMOS camera is used as photographed equipment. When
CMOS camera is installed on the machine tool, the distance
between the camera and the cutting tool should be adjusted to
acquire clear image. When taking pictures, the cutting tool is
moved to the ﬁxed shooting position by CNC lathe [ 19]. By adjust-
ing the camera ’s magnetic bracket, the camera lens optical axis is
adjusted perpendicular to the ﬂank face of the cutting tool. These
hardware parameters are presented in Table 1. To ensure the
quality of collected images and accuracy of detection systems, the
following two questions should be considered: the ﬁxed mounting
positions of bracket and camera and the cutting tool location
when capturing image.

5 Conclusions
In this article, the online detection system of ﬂank face wear
based on machine vision is designed, and an integrated image pro-
cessing algorithm to identify the ﬂank face wear is proposed. The
integrated image processing method is veri ﬁed by experiment. In
the actual production, the tool can be replaced according to the
actual state of tool wear. The research advantages of this research
can be summarized as follows:
– The system is simple and is easy to be built.
– The calculation time is short and has high calculation
efﬁciency.
– Micron-level accuracy can be achieved by the system.
The factors affecting the accuracy of the system and the solutions
are as follows:
– When taking photos, the verticality between the optical axis of
CMOS camera with the ﬂank face of cutting tool will affect the
system accuracy. To reduce the error caused by the camera
angle, we need to adjust the CMOS camera, and in the mea-
surement processing, the position of the tool should be
adjusted accurately.
– The effect of image noise. A very important issue of computer
image processing is the image noise. All captured images will
be introduced more or less noise, which impact the images
quality. Therefore, the image processes requires using the ﬁl-
tering methods to reduce image noise in this research and
the median ﬁlter operation is used to remove redundant pixel
noise of the binary image, which improves the accuracy of
the system.
